06 / PRESERVATION

The cards remain the cards.

The design reference does not recreate card faces, stacks, hands or gestures. The approved mock retains the copied shared components and their contracts.

Inspect the actual table ↗
  • One canonical face, rank and suit artwork.
  • Hold, drag, double activation and keyboard access.
  • Separate selection confirmation for pass and double.
  • Small Rally double tilt: −6% / 4°, normal overlap order.
  • Live-play last-trick pocket clear of the hand.
  • Pause, portrait and replay preserve the live state.
