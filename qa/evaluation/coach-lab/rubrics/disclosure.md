# Saved advice rubric

Apply these dimensions to the exact saved `coach-advice-2.0.0` presentation. Do not reconstruct a nicer reason from the final game.

| Dimension | Pass evidence | Failure or uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic purpose | The short cue gives the intended tactical purpose when the analysis supports one. | Generic play-low prose invents a benefit; a long paragraph obscures the cue; missing support remains uncertain. |
| Context grounding | The reason cites the actor's actual hand, public plays, remaining turns and objective where relevant. | Concealed holdings, future events or rank-absence claims are introduced without a valid deduction. |
| Action fidelity | Cue and reason describe the original selected legal action and policy version. | A later recomputation changes the action, context or explanation identity. |
| Uncertainty honesty | Exact facts are separated from conditional beliefs and policy preferences. | An inference or evaluator result is described as known ownership or proof of optimality. |
| Reason completeness | One Why request reveals the full saved explanation, alternatives and limitations. | A basic hint ladder delays the reason; one click reveals only another fragment. |

For forced or equivalent choices, an honest only-option/no-clear-preference message can pass disclosure. Do not demand an artificial tactical advantage. A missing strategic reason is an explanation gap; it is not an inaccurate-action label. Headless checks grade saved content. They do not prove browser delivery, click count, user comprehension or stronger play.
