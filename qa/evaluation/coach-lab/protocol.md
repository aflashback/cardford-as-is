# Lab operation and evidence protocol

Requirement family: LAB-01 through LAB-16 under CR-STRAT-011. Implementation evidence belongs in `implementation.md`; this protocol alone is not a passing test.

## Run identity and privacy

Freeze a private envelope containing literal family seeds, the exact selector/rules, per-seat deterministic policies, a complete source graph, worker binary hash and finite work/invocation caps. Preserve the graph's actual source bytes and worker in a bundle identified by those hashes. The runner verifies the bundle's physical bytes before and after execution. Resume uses the original manifest bytes and verifies the hash-chained journal. Keep all related arms, seat rotations and derived positions together as one original family for dataset separation and uncertainty.

The authoritative journal is private. Each actor receives only their permitted observation and complete legal choices. Review exports are constructed through allowlists and contain opaque case IDs. Original analyses/cues, later public outcomes, hidden hands and fixture answers cannot enter blind stage A. A full-game retrospective remains separate from decision-time evidence.

The source/binary pin identifies reproducibility, not strategic strength. A public descriptor does not establish actual seed custody. Confirmation remains unavailable until independent labels, custody, study choices and the existing strength gates are met.

## Execution and recovery

An original preparation is persisted before its action is committed. An illegal/mismatched preparation fails the trajectory with no substituted card. Atomic sealed choices are prepared from the same pre-resolution state. Native actor, sealed, automatic, chance, round-complete and session-complete nodes own the lifecycle and score.

The worker checkpoints on its cooperative work deadline or a cancellation it can handle. The outer watchdog bounds the owned process tree and reserves startup/shutdown time; a forced kill may leave a stale lock or torn journal and is not a verified checkpoint. Work limits and missing outcomes remain visible. A future resource expansion is a new declared run; it must not retroactively erase censoring or source drift. Reports count every admitted arm, including ones that could not start before the deadline.

For an abandoned lock, first read `private/owner.lock`, verify that the exact recorded process is no longer running, and keep a copy of the lock with recovery notes. Only then remove that single stale lock and request normal verified resume. Never delete the archive or a lock belonging to a running process. Do not automatically repair a partial journal line: archive it for investigation and use the last independently verified checkpoint as a separately identified recovery, not an unchanged successful run.

## Review states

Code owns legal actions, scores, winners and forced status. Strategic verdicts are supported, inferior, uncertain or not-applicable; pending has no verdict. Explanation factuality, strategic relevance, realized results and human learning are separate dimensions.

Stage A is blind to the saved action and wording. Freeze it before stage B. First assess the cue alone, then the cue plus its complete saved reason; a persuasive Why cannot repair a misleading initial cue. Stage C may discuss later public events, but cannot rewrite A/B as if the actor knew them in advance. Missing coverage and ambiguous judgments remain explicit.

Fixture review proves serialization, ordering, accounting and failure behavior only. Imported model reviews retain `llm-proposal` provenance. Independent human submissions retain qualifications, exposure and their original accepted set; disputes are not silently averaged into truth. No calibration percentage is reported without its actual eligible denominator.

## Changes and release

Each finding retains original evidence, version and family references. Distinguish hypotheses, independently supported findings, disputed findings and reproduced regressions. A cue-only fix must preserve the selected action. A policy candidate needs an observable trigger, exceptions, positive and negative fixtures and independent evidence or an applicable executable proof.

Do not automatically install candidates, tune handwritten likelihoods on held-out evidence or train a runtime model. Freeze one deterministic candidate, compare complete trajectories on fresh families, retain subgroup harm and missing outcomes, and apply the existing owner/strength gates before promotion. Bots never call an AI model.
