## Stage 1: short cue comprehension

Recruitment follows a recorded consent and privacy plan. Record the participant's experience with the exact variant/rule profile. Show short Notice → Consider → A suggestion cues on reachable actor-safe positions. Ask what fact the cue describes, which inference remains uncertain, and what action it suggests. Capture the participant's own explanation before feedback. Independently qualified reviewers score factual understanding, unsafe hidden-card inference, misleading certainty, abstention and confusion. Separate exact fact cues from heuristic preferences such as offering partner a ruff. An unfamiliar participant's agreement with the suggested card does not prove comprehension.

Use one actor's trajectory chronologically, seal each response before any later event and withhold other-seat packets, later play, recommendations in blind tests, outcomes, seeds and related mode/deal twins. If exposure isolation cannot be proved, assign one case per family. Track exactly which cues, source packets, answers and later cards each person saw; exposed cases cannot count as independent blind labels. Preserve original reviewer copies before adjudication and keep unresolved unknown/contested states.

This stage is usability preparation. Do not extend it into a learning claim or reuse exposed cases as unfamiliar posttests.

## Stage 2: immediate and delayed unaided transfer

After labels and study decisions freeze, allocate participants to coached and uncoached practice with equal time, rule access and difficulty. Stratify by recorded pretest skill; conceal allocation until baseline is collected, and blind outcome scorers. Counterbalance independently reviewed parallel forms. Practice and pretest/immediate/delayed test positions must come from explicitly separated unfamiliar families, with matched mode twins/rotations kept in their assigned family.

The proposed delayed visit is 7–14 days after practice; the exact interval/window must be fixed in the confirming protocol. Both immediate and delayed tests are unaided: no hints, coach, policy answer, exposed retry, screenshots of previous answers, hidden hands or future history. Use unfamiliar fixed partners/opponents in Partners and record their policy pins. Technical assistance, uncertain external assistance, missed visits and late visits remain separate observations rather than being relabeled unaided.

Proposed primary endpoint per variant is the baseline-adjusted coached-versus-uncoached difference in delayed unfamiliar acceptable-action score. Actual acceptable sets and unresolved unknown/contested cases determine assessable denominators. Secondary outcomes include immediate unaided decisions, explanation of public evidence, retention, time and abstention; report misleading-cue uptake and harmful advice separately. Match wins, coach agreement and satisfaction do not establish learning. Freeze useful gain/harm bounds, multiplicity and sample-size rationale before confirming enrollment; a small comprehension pilot supplies no efficacy result.

Analyze by randomized assignment with participant-level uncertainty and family exposure tracked. Predeclare attrition by arm, interrupted visits and missing-data sensitivity analysis. Forced moves are logged but excluded from strategic-choice/learning denominators. Record exactly where consent is revoked or assistance/exposure compromises a visit.
