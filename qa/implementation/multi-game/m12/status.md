## Test cost and stopping discipline

Recorded full-hand browser journeys take approximately **1.9–2.3 minutes** while stepping real rendered turns and waiting for UI progression/animations. This is distinct from a pure engine test. The experimental exhaustive Hearts sealed pass is also expensive: all four actors completed two-world/all-286-candidate preparation in **59.574 seconds** before one atomic commit. Neither measurement establishes production latency percentiles or capacity.

Earlier broad normal/race suites took 6m04s/19m28s, and the old unsuccessful 57-minute browser attempts were overbroad diagnostics. They are retained as history, not a reason to repeat unchanged full soaks after each edit. The new native owned-process-tree watchdog caps the **entire invoked run, including multiple binaries and shutdown**, at 30 minutes. A stopped run leaves pending checks **unverified**; an incomplete hand/match cannot become a completed outcome or acceptance claim. Use focused selections and retain failed/censored captures; do not silently extend, resume or rerun a comparison to manufacture coverage.
