## Implemented behavior

- Hearts, both Spades modes and all four Trix variants use the same proportional `CardFace`. Hand, trick, Jack board, announcements and last trick change only size; context-specific face typography and decoration have been removed.
- The human uses the shared `PlayerBadge` at the centred South table edge, matching the opponents. The held hand occupies a separate table-aligned row. All four remaining-card fans sit within their badges.
- Doubling uses a small non-overlay `Double?` control and actual-hand selections. Noneligible cards remain fully coloured and read-only. Confirmation and phase changes clear selection, so doubled cards do not stay raised when play begins. Read-only announced cards are 20% larger than the prior design and disappear when publicly played.
- Request completion continues automatically after the final four-card hold. No automatic score/result popup opens. Current points are beside each player; manually opened Scores contains only Current / Kingdom / Total.
- Score history enables after a request completes. Opening fetches the completed-round index; selecting a round fetches its frozen breakdown. Authenticated native reads reuse the existing persisted ledger and engine arithmetic. Live table, presentation and actor transport copies carry no ledger or withheld-bonus history; original native observations and saved decisions retain both.
