2. An assignment manager uses the private source index. Assign one actor trajectory per underlying deal, chronologically, sealing each verdict before later events. Prevent cross-seat, mode-twin, rotated-deal and future-history exposure before original labels freeze. If isolation cannot be proved, assign one case per family. Flag exposed reviews and exclude them from blind measures. Two people inspecting all 32 packets cannot establish 32 independent blind reviews; obtain fresh deals or additional unexposed reviewers. Randomize only independent assignments.
3. Supply only actor hand, permitted history, public state, rules/objective and legal actions. Conceal source identities, policies, recommendations, other hands and outcomes. Record reviewer experience/profile understanding. Seek two independent competent reviews: acceptable alternatives, unacceptable actions/reasons, assumptions, facts and counterexamples. Labels: `supported`, `unknown`, `contested`; unknown is not an empty acceptable set. Preserve originals before third-reviewer adjudication; unresolved disagreement stays contested.
4. Report acceptable-set agreement on resolved cases, harmful advice on assessable cases, and unknown/contested/exposed rates on all cases, with family/phase/mode counts and denominators. Assess cue factuality, misleading implications and abstention separately. Agent agreement or later outcomes supply no independent answer key.

For exact small endgames, declare the objective, enumerate allocations consistent with actor knowledge, and enforce identical choices at indistinguishable future information sets. Prove claims across feasible worlds or report assumption-dependent bounds; separate review code verifies proofs. Solving the actual four-hand deal is not an actor-safe answer key.
