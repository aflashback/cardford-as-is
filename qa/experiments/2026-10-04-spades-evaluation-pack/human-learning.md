# Human learning study instructions

Proposed study; no participants recruited, contacted or measured. Freeze consent, eligibility, variant, content, allocation seed, scoring rubric, timing, exclusions and sample plan before enrollment. Document the population; experience with other card games does not establish Spades expertise.

Randomize participants, stratified by pretest ability, to coached versus uncoached practice with equal time, rules access and difficulty. Keep assignment concealed until baseline is recorded; blind outcome scorers. Use independently reviewed, parallel pretest and immediate posttest forms. A delayed test after 7–14 days uses novel deals/families and unfamiliar fixed teammates. Tests are unaided; do not show hints, retry exposed positions, or reuse training answers. Counterbalance test forms.

Primary outcome per variant: baseline-adjusted difference between arms in delayed novel unaided acceptable-action score. Independently score understanding of public evidence and transfer; measure misleading-cue uptake, abstention/helpfulness, time and retention separately. Log hint exposure, retries, future-card exposure and technical failures. Mark assisted/exposed decisions; report their counts and never relabel them unaided.

Analyze by randomized assignment, with participant-level uncertainty, attrition by arm and a predeclared missing-data sensitivity analysis. Report adherence separately. Freeze a meaningful learning-effect target and power/precision calculation using credible variance estimates before a confirming study; the small usability pilot cannot establish educational efficacy. Match wins, satisfaction and agreement with the coach are secondary observations, not learning proof.
