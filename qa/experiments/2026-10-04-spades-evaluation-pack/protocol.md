## Freeze before execution

Freeze `pilot/pilot-manifest.json`: source commit/hashes pin exact rule, engine, adapter, generator and metric code; allocation pins ordered cells, seat policies and hand limits. This protocol pins the time budget, metrics and exclusions. Reference: frozen `spades-tactics-2.1.0`; candidate: existing `spades-cases-4.0.0`. Hash every opponent/teammate implementation. Use only development styles (`d0`, `baseline`, `cautious-contract`, `aggressive-setter`, `nil-seeker`, `bag-dodger`); freeze contrasting combinations and unfamiliar fixed teammates. “Unfamiliar” means a distinct fixed teammate policy, not an unseen human population. No held-out styles or sealed confirmation seeds may run. Record pilot seed provenance in its manifest; never quietly extend a cap after inspecting outcomes.

Pilot allocation: eight families, seeds 8,000,001–8,000,008 from open `matches-development`; two modes × four rotations × two fixed environments × two arms = 256 planned streams. Limit streams to 100 hands; use a cooperative 20-minute simulation/replay budget. Serialization and nonpreemptible calls can add overhead. Save checkpoints; resume only under the unchanged manifest. Runtime interruption is a resource stop, not an efficacy stop. The manifest specifies each environment's relative-seat policies.

## Paired full matches

Run Solo and Partners separately. Replace exactly one seat with the evaluated arm, rotate through all four seats, and keep other-seat policies identical between paired arms. Start at zero score/bags with identical seeded deal streams and dealer; advance through production transitions, retaining scores/bags and rotating the dealer. Allow trajectories and match lengths to diverge. Only the production terminal result supplies a winner.

Cluster by initial stream seed across rotations, styles, teammates and both modes. Match hands and decisions are dependent observations. Give frozen cells equal weight within each seed. For each mode, describe paired terminal win-indicator differences and final margin differences: Solo own points minus mean opponent points; Partners team minus opposing team. Show own/team scores, hands, contract and Nil attempts/outcomes, bags, penalties, action divergence, decision latency/work, and coverage denominators. Stratify bidding/play and style; do not merge the variants.

Capped/interrupted matches are censored, with last state and reason retained; unstarted streams remain `not-run`. Report planned, paired-observed, terminal and hand-capped counts by arm/cell. These counts exclude interrupted pairs; their partial journals and any finished reference arm remain diagnostic evidence. Complete-pair summaries are conditional on completion and may be biased; show win-difference bounds treating censored outcomes as unknown. Scores including capped arms describe only the bounded observation horizon. No capped score becomes a winner. Tiny-pilot intervals are descriptive; no p-value, pass label, or early efficacy stopping. Stop on safety failure; preserve errors and seeds. An unperformed safety/cue check is `not-run`, never zero errors. Report factual-check coverage and abstention against every unforced evaluated decision where measured.

## Confirmation remains separate

Pilot variance, censoring and runtime inform a prospective sample-size/precision plan; a small observed effect cannot justify claimed power, and zero changes cannot yield a sample-size estimate. Commit a complete manifest before reading fresh confirmation data. [confirmation-template.json](confirmation-template.json) is deliberately non-executable.

Existing SG criteria apply only to their original in-match **per-hand** estimands and Partners **both-teammates** placement: safety violation → Blocked; proven decision parity → Unchanged; interval upper <0 → Worse; lower >0 → Improved; interval contains zero and lower >−δ → Non-inferior (δ=2.5 Solo, 5 Partners); otherwise Inconclusive. Favorable statuses need ≥200 changed seeds and ≥500 changed decisions. Promotion needs targeted-mode Improved, other-mode Improved/Non-inferior/Unchanged, own-score and adjusted subgroup guards, baseline harm-floor reporting, competent human labels/owner waiver and recorded owner approval. Use seed-clustered inference, 10,000 fixed-seed resamples and predeclared multiplicity. A single-seat full-match confirmation needs a new approved estimand, harm bounds and change record; per-hand thresholds cannot be transferred. This pilot cannot change defaults.
