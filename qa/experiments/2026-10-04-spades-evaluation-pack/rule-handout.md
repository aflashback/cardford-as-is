# Reviewer rule handout

This describes the frozen CardFord training profiles, not every house version of Spades. Use it with one assigned packet. Card IDs use suit initials and ranks 2–10/J/Q/K/A; Ace is highest. Seats 0–3 run clockwise. In Partners, 0/2 and 1/3 are teammates. In Solo, every other seat is an opponent.

- Everyone bids once, 0–13. Zero means Nil: promise to win no tricks. Other bids promise that many tricks; already announced bids are public.
- Follow the led suit when possible. Otherwise play any held card; taking the trick or using a Spade is optional. Before a Spade has been played, lead another suit unless only Spades remain.
- A trick goes to its highest Spade, or its highest card of the led suit if no Spade was played. The winner leads next.
- In Solo, count each player's positive bid and tricks separately. In Partners, add the team's positive bids and tricks won by its positive bidders. A Nil player's tricks never help that positive promise.
- Making a positive promise earns 10 points per promised trick plus 1 per extra trick. Missing it loses 10 points per promised trick. A successful Nil adds 100; a failed Nil loses 100.
- Extra tricks add bags. Failed-Nil tricks also add bags, but earn no extra-trick points. Every ten accumulated bags cost 100 points; the remainder carries forward.
- After a complete hand, a competitor at 500 or more can win if it has the unique highest score. A tied highest score continues. Negative scores are allowed; a testing hand limit does not determine a winner.

Aim to win under those scoring rules while using only your own cards and permitted public history. The complete legal action list is supplied. Reviews may accept several reasonable choices or remain uncertain; do not assume an unseen card belongs to anyone because of an outcome alone.
