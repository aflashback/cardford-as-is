# Trix frontier candidate: fixed development protocol

Status: the predeclared v2 experiment completed on 9 October 2026: 64 development and 64 fresh-validation sessions, 1,792 deals and all 64 planned pairs. Descriptors were initialized against settled source before episodes; the unchanged-source full gate passed before collection. Both stages used the same frozen rule/source/binary. This is a bounded development experiment for `trix-frontier-1.0.0`, not automatic promotion or a claim of stronger play. The candidate inherits the history policy; the comparison control is frozen `trix-history-1.0.0` on the same native profile and source graph, so the treatment differs only by the frontier rule. The concrete deterministic rule was recorded with its implementation before initialization.

The fixed hypothesis applies only to unforced Partners Jacks layout turns. After a legal root opens a suit frontier, retain the immediately reachable own chain; remove three inherited heuristic points for each distant own card beyond the first external/missing card on that branch. Preserve the inherited adjacent-card credit (+5), reachable contiguous-card credit (+3), jack-opening cost (−1), finishing value (1,000), exact partner/opponent ownership weights and deterministic tie handling. Existing history policy behavior supplies all other phases and Solo. This corrects a concrete bottleneck assumption without claiming unknown-card ownership or calibrating hidden behavior. Actor-only `frontierEvidence` version `trix-frontier-evidence-1.0.0` records coverage (`outside-scope`, `forced-layout`, `compared`), eligibility, inherited `priorCandidateId`, `selectedChanged` and each legal candidate's inherited/new value, removed credit and branches (direction, owned chain, blocked own cards, first external card, possible holders).

The saved J♦/10♠ investigation supplies a hypothesis and eight selected harmful nearby cases. It does not supply an acceptable-action label or a prevalence estimate. A rule can accept some conditional losses while seeking higher expected team score, but the conditional sampler is not calibrated. Full sessions therefore measure focal-team score margin; actor finishing position alone is not the objective. The prior history pilot completed 128 sessions and changed no choices, so this candidate must separately report eligibility, actual activation and costly failures.

## Pairing, objective and analysis

Each arm installs its policy at logical seat 0 for one-seat deployment, or logical seats 0/2 for both teammates. Rotation 1 exercises the other physical team. The other three or two seats use either `trix-deterministic-1.0.0` throughout or `trix-reasoning-1.0.0` throughout. These are two existing deterministic environments; they do not establish robustness to unfamiliar human players or broader partner styles.

Primary outcome is candidate minus reference focal-team full-session score margin, averaged across the two rotations within each origin family. Both native arms must complete both rotations before a family contributes an uncertainty value. Basic/Complex, environments, deployment modes and stages remain separate. Report every planned pair, candidate/reference W/T/L, each signed paired margin, family means, worst paired loss and descriptive bootstrap intervals. Do not treat moves, requests, games, rotations, selected counterexamples or repeated worlds as independent samples.

The existing study summary uses a deterministic 4,000-resample family percentile bootstrap with cohort alpha `0.05/4`; it is exploratory and has no power guarantee. Configs preserve useful gain 5, maximum harm 0 and minimum 20 families. Two families cannot meet that floor even with a positive mean, so every cohort remains `insufficient-evidence`, `strengthClaim: false` and `promotionEligible: false`. The twenty-family floor itself is not a power calculation. No pooled headline or equivalence claim is permitted from a small or degenerate interval.

Read frontier activation only from retained decision-time traces in the matching admitted export: layout eligibility, rule application, `priorCandidateId` and `selectedChanged` relative to the saved history control, forced choices, candidate bottleneck features and fallback reasons. Summarize by variant/environment/deployment, origin family and declared tactical scope. Missing frontier trace evidence is unavailable, never zero. The existing history-specific summary must not be relabelled frontier activation. A changed choice is behavior coverage, not a correctness label. When a paired session loses, preserve its exact receipt and decision references; subsequent inspection is diagnostic and cannot erase it from the denominator or change the frozen rule.
