# CFL-09: opening and delaying through a native Jacks finish

Owner continuation, 9 October 2026: “lets start the next steps now” starts the next stage after [exact twos deductions](trix-ownership.md). Build a reusable, headless comparison of small Jacks endings and preserve concrete cases before deciding whether a further deterministic rule is justified. The bot/coach remains deterministic; no model, UI or live-default change is part of this stage.

## Comparison boundary

The input is one player's observation and its exact installed pin. For exhaustive allocation coverage, require the current available chronological public prefix. Enumerate remaining-card assignments consistent with the actor's hand, public counts, placements, passes and announcements, and replay each through the native engine. A private fixture deal proves reachability but must never be the evaluator's sole hidden-state oracle.

Compare every legal root action on every retained allocation under the same declared deterministic continuation profiles. Each future decision receives only that actor's observation. A profile may place the ownership candidate at one seat, both teammates, or all seats; the frozen History control remains explicit. Allocation counts and conditional averages are not calibrated probabilities. Do not select a different perfect-information future plan for each hidden allocation.

The first implementation has explicit limits on total remaining cards, allocations, reconstruction work, native steps and profile count. Exhausting any limit leaves the comparison incomplete; a deterministic prefix of allocations must not be presented as a representative sample or exact ranking. Do not publish a partly compared allocation. Unsupported historical/early observations remain outside this exact-ending scope; the existing sampled request evaluator remains available separately.

## Outcomes and timing

For each complete root/profile/allocation, retain all four native finishing positions and seat awards, actor points, partner/team points where applicable, and a separately labelled score margin. Native Jacks awards total 500. In Partners, a 50-point team gain corresponds to a 100-point margin gain. Solo optimizes the actor's own points; it has no partner or Partners twos information.

Retain the legal action sequence, passes and remaining counts. Distinguish when a card becomes playable on the board from when a player actually plays it. Board distance or an opening offset is not a prediction of turns to finish. Use native turn order, finished-seat skips and completion rules. Record whether future choices were forced: complete feasible-allocation comparisons with all future choices forced have a stronger local interpretation than results conditional on a selected continuation policy.

## Cases and interpretation

Use fixed native legal prefixes for a partner with several/all/no twos, releasing a partner's low path while retaining an upper blocker, a faster individual finish that lowers combined team points, an equal-team-score contrast, and corresponding Solo tradeoffs. Include adverse/neutral cases and continuation sensitivity. Selected diagnostic fixtures do not estimate prevalence or establish complete-session strength.

Only a supported narrow comparison should become a new candidate rule. First check whether every feasible allocation supports it, whether future choices are forced or depend on policy assumptions, and whether the opposite objective in Solo reverses the result. A general numerical bonus for helping a two or delaying an ace is not justified by one positive fixture. Any subsequent candidate requires a separate identity, saved bot/coach evidence and validation against frozen History on fresh origins.

## Delivery and checks

Add the native offline evaluator with independent scoring/replay/budget regressions, a fixed case corpus and a bounded local operator that preserves source bytes, compiled worker, invocation receipts and a readable report. The operator must refuse to overwrite evidence and must mark failed, interrupted, incomplete or changed-source runs as unverified.

Verification proceeds from focused native and operator tests to the installed `npm run lab:check` gate and one fixed case-corpus run. Results, exact commands, interpretation and remaining work belong in [the acceptance record](../../../qa/evaluation/coach-lab/trix-jacks-endings-acceptance.md).
