# Reasoning interviewer — answers and challenge

4 October 2026; replies to the architecture interviewer's actual six questions in `01-architecture-position.md`. Opening reasoning position and RQ1–RQ6 are preserved in `02-reasoning-position.md`. Investigation only.

## Answers A1–A6

**A1 — minimum protocol.** Each installed game supplies closed observation/action schemas; complete legal candidates with stable semantic IDs; information/phase/objective versions; deterministic action effects; and a decision record identifying source, selected action, candidate coverage and limitations. Claims must name module-owned predicates and distinguish exact, heuristic and sampled evidence. Sampling metadata is required only when sampling occurs. No compulsory trick/bid fields, plan, scalar confidence or preferred action when evidence cannot choose. The shared envelope carries identity, actor, competition, lifecycle, versions and provenance.

**A2 — ownership of reasoning.** Share validation, budgets, reproducible random streams, common-world comparisons, statistical arithmetic, trace persistence and claim-dispatch machinery. Keep rule deductions, ownership constraints, likelihood models, objectives, tactics and proof predicates game-owned. Tarneeb auction strength is an interpretation of a public commitment; an auction bid cannot exclude a holding unless its reviewed rules prove that exclusion. Trix contract availability is exact; expected performance under a chosen contract is policy-dependent. Public exposure through doubling can change exact knowledge only under the selected rule profile. Version uncalibrated assumptions; never turn weights into player-facing probabilities.

**A3 — information gates.** A simulator may hold a complete synthetic state, but continuation policies should receive a projected actor input. Require indistinguishable actor views to yield identical policy inputs/actions, hidden-hand permutation tests, legal/reachable simulations and production-scorer parity. For tree search, future choices must share information-set decisions across worlds; independently optimizing each perfect-information world introduces strategy fusion. Existing flat continuations already restrict reads to own/public information, but the generic `defaultMove(world)` signature relies on discipline/tests rather than structural exclusion. Preserve the actual deal outside all search inputs. Sampling uncertainty does not bound model bias.

**A4 — policy selection prerequisites.** Freeze each variant's exact rules, competitors, score orientation, terminal ranking, decision horizon and strategic comparison rule first. Obtain independent acceptable-action sets with contested/unknown labels, tractable exact reference positions where feasible, and fresh grouped deal families against contrasting opponents and unfamiliar teammates. Separate auction/contract-selection/play/layout performance; report harm, factual accuracy, abstention, compute and latency alongside outcomes. Existing Spades gate machinery is reusable, its game-specific estimand/constants are not. Neither familiar browser outcomes nor Jev agreement selects the strongest algorithm. Compare explicit tactical/flat-rollout policies first; investigate ISMCTS only after identifiable failures. CFR's two-player zero-sum guarantee does not transfer automatically to decentralized partnerships.

**A5 — plan memory.** Persist an actor-owned, versioned belief/plan snapshot only from that actor's legal information, with public event references and declared assumptions. Prefer deterministic reconstruction where sufficient; current Spades plans are reconstructed from observation. Never share private plans between partners. Historical review reads the original snapshot/advice; recomputation belongs to a separately identified analysis. A Trix contract pool and kingdom state may be public memory, while the player's private holdings and inferred intentions retain their own visibility rules.

**A6 — rewrite threshold.** Rewrite the engine/planner selectively if new legal phases, information changes or horizons require fake Spades fields, duplicated authoritative reducers, unsafe views or materially wrong selection semantics. A whole-app rewrite needs evidence that these defects cannot be isolated while retaining access, idempotency, persistence and audit behavior. Preserve original schemas/records with versioned legacy dispatch or explicit read-only support; never reinterpret old utilities, advice or scores.

## Challenge C1 and counterquestions

I agree with extracting a production registry and preserving proven orchestration. I challenge retaining `PlanningPack` wholesale: its `actor=null`/utility boundary is round-terminal, and `withStance` fixes intent for that round. In a Trix profile where choosing a contract consumes it from a kingdom's remaining pool, immediate negative-trick value can conflict with later layout/finish-order value. Calling the first hand terminal hides that tradeoff. The exact variant remains unresolved; this is a design counterexample, not adopted rules.

RQ1–RQ6 were sent verbatim to the architect. They ask about production dispatch, multi-phase horizons, game-owned hard/soft knowledge, actor-view isolation, payoff vectors/comparison rules, and evidence for rewrite with historical compatibility. Preserve reusable budget/evidence arithmetic while testing whether the full planner API fits.

**Closing:** The architect accepted C1 and revised reuse to budget/pairing/evidence components. I accept that revision; production horizon/reveal tests and actor projection remain prerequisites. Responses are preserved in `03-architecture-answers.md`.
