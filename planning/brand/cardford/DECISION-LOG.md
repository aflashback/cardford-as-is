# Cardford brand decision log

| Date | Decision | Reason and effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-26 | Owner chose Tricks Coach and `trickscoach.com` ([CR-BRAND-001](../../brand-and-domain.md)). | Coaching was explicit. Domain addition to Vercel was planned, but no live verification was recorded. This is now historical. |
| 2026-09-27–28 | Owner reconsidered coaching-led naming and compared Cardford with other card-game names. | The product may attract returning players for games, bots, progress and design, including when they do not want a lesson. A broad card-game name preserves that room. |
| 2026-09-28 | Owner selected the first Cardford “Open Hand” visual direction. | Three fanned playing cards make the category immediate; the full word Cardford stays prominent and avoids a Ford-only reading. Four-suit and card-corner alternatives were shown but not selected. |
| 2026-09-28 | Owner removed “Stay for another hand.” from the banner and the final period from the motto. | The approved visible motto is exactly **Card games. Your way**. The selected raster board is [`cardford-open-hand-selected.png`](../../cardford-open-hand-selected.png). |
| 2026-09-28 | Created a reproducible SVG family from the selected raster direction. | Solid vectors provide scalable logo, stamp, icon, header and banner files. The project's existing SIL-OFL Manrope font is embedded in text-bearing assets. This is an interpretation of the selected concept, not a pixel trace. |

**Current scope:** the selected visual direction is the three-card Cardford artwork. Technical Jev identifiers and historical records retain their existing names. A separate display-copy change already put **CardFord** in the current application; these SVG assets have not been installed there. The capitalization difference between that copy and the selected artwork is being resolved before the asset set is treated as the final spelling. Neither `cardford.com` registration nor either domain's Vercel status was checked during this asset work.
